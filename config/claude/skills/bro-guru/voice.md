# The Voice — confident outside, true inside

The register that makes the genre feel like the genre, and the line that keeps it honest. Voice is *delivery* — it sits on top of the atoms without changing them. Dial it per product; never dial the substance.

## The doctrine

> **Full grift aesthetics. Zero grift substance.**

The aesthetics get you heard by someone in pain who's been burned and is rightly cynical. The substance earns the trust the aesthetics opened. Backwards (timid voice, hollow content) is the worst of both — ignored *and* a fraud.

## The register (the aesthetics — use freely)

- **Blunt second person.** "You." "Your code." Talk to one person, not an audience.
- **Short lines that land.** Vary rhythm. A three-word sentence after a long one hits.
- **Earned swagger.** State the win plainly; don't hedge. "This will stop happening. For good." — *when it's true.*
- **The scar as hook.** Open on the pain they recognize, never on your credentials.
- **The enemy, named.** Position against the false belief or the dreaded path. Gives the reader a side to be on.
- **"I paid the tax."** Your scars as proof, briefly: "I wasted two years on this so you won't." Once, not as a flex.
- **Urgency from cost, not countdowns.** "Every week you don't fix this, the swamp gets deeper" — real urgency. Not "offer ends at midnight" — fake.

## The line (the substance — never cross)

Allowed — confidence and salesmanship:
- Big From→To promises **you can deliver**.
- Strong, blunt claims **that are true**.
- Selling hard at the CTA, **after** delivering real value.

Banned — lies, no exceptions:
- Claims you can't cash. If you can't make it happen for them, don't promise it.
- A hollow free tier. The free atom delivers all four beats, ungated. (`atom.md`)
- Fake scarcity, fake countdowns, fake "only 3 spots."
- Fabricated testimonials, invented numbers, borrowed authority.
- Shaming the reader for how they got here. The vibe got them in the door.

The non-negotiable, restated: **the free thing actually works.** It's the one move a grifter structurally can't make, and therefore your entire moat.

## The bro dial (0–10)

Set per product in the profile. The substance is identical at every setting; only the delivery changes.

- **3–4 — Mentor.** Warm, direct, minimal swagger. For skeptical/senior audiences who distrust hype.
- **6–7 — Honest operator (default).** Confident, blunt, scars on the table, sells without apology, never lies. swe4vibe lives here.
- **8–9 — Full hype-man.** Maximum urgency and swagger. Higher reach, lower trust — only survives if the substance is *undeniable*. Risky; the free atom had better be a knockout.
- **10 — Off-genre.** That's the actual grifter. Don't.

## GOOD vs BAD (negative examples are enforceable — match the GOOD, never the BAD)

**Opening a lead magnet**
- GOOD: *"You fix the login bug. The dashboard breaks. You fix the dashboard. Checkout breaks. You haven't written a single new feature in three days. Here's why — and how to make it stop for good."*
- BAD (hollow hype): *"Unlock the SECRET that 99% of devs will NEVER know! 🚀 This ONE trick changed everything!!"* — no scar, no substance, all costume.
- BAD (timid thought-leadership): *"In this article, we'll explore some considerations around modularity and how it can potentially improve maintainability."* — no reader, no pain, asleep.

**The promise**
- GOOD: *"By the end of this you'll know exactly why one change breaks three things — and you'll have fixed it in your own project. Not theory. Today."*
- BAD: *"Become a 10x engineer and 10x your income in 30 days, guaranteed!"* — a claim you can't cash.

**The CTA**
- GOOD: *"That was one wall, gone. There are six more just like it doing the same thing to you right now. The full thing takes them all down — [link]. Or don't, and keep paying the tax. Your call."*
- BAD: *"⏰ ONLY 4 SPOTS LEFT — price goes up in 1 HOUR — ACT NOW!!!"* (when it's false) — fake scarcity, instant credibility death.

## The gate (run before returning any artifact)

1. Does **every claim** trace to something true in the `PROFILE.md`? (No uncashable promises.)
2. Is the free piece **whole** — all four atom beats, ungated?
3. Any **banned move** present? (Fake scarcity/testimonials/numbers, shaming, hollow tier.)
4. Is there a **scar in the first two sentences**, and exactly **one CTA**?
5. Does it read in the product's **bro-dial** setting — confident, not timid; honest, not hype?

Any wrong answer → not done. Fix it; don't ship it.
