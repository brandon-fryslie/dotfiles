# The Bro-Guru Info Product, decomposed

The genre is a machine that converts a stranger's **pain** into a **purchase**, and — in the honest version — the purchase into a **real win**. Like any well-built thing it's a set of parts joined at seams. Cut it at its real joints and every part has one job. There are two assemblies: the **substance core** (what's actually true and useful) and the **funnel shell** (what converts strangers into buyers). The core is why it's worth buying. The shell is why anyone does.

---

## The substance core

**The atom.** The irreducible unit of value: one *pain → its true name → the model that dissolves it → proof you can feel*. Everything sellable is made of these. Fully specified in `atom.md`. A product with no real atoms is a costume; a product with real atoms and no shell is a secret.

**The curriculum.** An ordered stack of atoms. The order is a teaching sequence, and choosing it is a real decision (logical dependency vs. leading with the worst pain). The stack's *size* is the scope — knowable and bounded, not open-ended. The curriculum is the spine of the product; the shell hangs off it.

---

## The funnel shell

Seven parts. Each is a separate seam — change one without disturbing the others.

1. **The Mark's Moment** — the precise instant of pain when the buyer is *ready*. You never sell to someone who isn't at the moment; you sell to the moment. Name it concretely (swe4vibe: "the wall" — when vibes stop working and they lack the words to say why). Everything upstream exists to find people at this moment; everything downstream exists to convert them while they're in it.

2. **The Enemy** — the false belief or failed alternative you position against. Every guru product needs one. It's what the buyer was told that didn't work, or the over-expensive/over-slow "proper" path they're dreading. ("You don't need a CS degree." "The other gurus are selling maps they don't have.") The enemy makes the promise *necessary* — without it you're a nice-to-have.

3. **The Promise** — the transformation, stated as **From → To**, concrete, ideally time-bound. Not "learn X" but "stop hitting the wall, and never hit it the same way twice." The promise is a *claim about the buyer's future*, and the proof mechanism is what makes the claim believable.

4. **The Proof Mechanism** — why you're credible *and* not a grifter. This is the genre's load-bearing wall and where almost everyone fails. The honest move: **give one real win away free** — one whole atom that actually works. A grifter can't afford this (a hollow free tier is structural for them); you can, and it's the entire moat. Secondary proof: your own scars, real artifacts, real before/afters. Banned proof: fake testimonials, fabricated numbers, borrowed authority you don't have.

5. **The Voice** — confident outside, true inside. The register that makes the genre feel like the genre. Fully specified in `voice.md`. The voice is *delivery*, not substance — it can be dialed up or down per product without touching the atoms.

6. **The Offer Ladder** — tiers, cheapest to dearest: **free** (the lead-magnet atom — proof) → **core** (the curriculum — the product) → **premium** (cohort, community, templates, 1:1 — leverage on the same atoms). Each rung is the same substance at a different level of hand-holding. Don't invent new substance per rung; repackage access to it.

7. **The Channels** — where teasers live (long-form posts, social threads, short video, email). Each teaser is a **truncated atom**: the scar as hook, one beat of real value, a call-to-action to the free thing. Channels point at the lead magnet; the lead magnet points at the offer; the offer points at the ladder. One direction, no loops.

---

## The honest/grift axis — the only ethical dial that matters

The genre's *form* is amoral. The same funnel shell sells a life-changing skill and a $50 PDF of nothing. What separates "grift" from "lesson I'd swear by" is exactly one thing: **whether the substance core is real.** So the doctrine is simple and absolute:

> **Full grift aesthetics. Zero grift substance.**

The aesthetics (urgency, swagger, the enemy, the bold promise) are how you reach someone in pain who's been burned before and is rightly cynical. The substance (atoms that actually work, a free win that actually wins) is what earns the trust the aesthetics opened the door for. Get these backwards — timid voice, hollow content — and you're the worst of both: ignored *and* a fraud. The genre, done right, is generous wearing a salesman's jacket.

## How the parts compose

```
                 CHANNELS  (teasers = truncated atoms)
                     │  point at
                     ▼
   PROOF ──────►  LEAD MAGNET  (one whole free atom)
   (free win)         │  bridges to
                      ▼
   ENEMY ─► PROMISE ─► OFFER ──► LADDER (free → core → premium)
                      ▲              ▲
                      │              │
              all built from   THE CURRICULUM
              the same ──────► (ordered stack of ATOMS)
                                     ▲
                            each atom anchored to
                            THE MARK'S MOMENT
```

Read it once. Then never re-derive it per piece of content — that's what the `PROFILE.md` is for. The profile is this anatomy, *answered* for one specific product.
