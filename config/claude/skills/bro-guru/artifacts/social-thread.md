# Artifact: Social Thread / Short-Form

> One of four capped channel templates. Covers all *short, hook-driven, feed-native* teasers — X/Twitter threads, LinkedIn posts, and short-video scripts (Reels/Shorts/TikTok) are the same shape at different lengths. A short-video script is this with [VISUAL] cues; don't make it a separate mode.

**What it is:** a **truncated atom** built to stop the scroll and send traffic to the free lead magnet. It delivers *one* real beat of value — enough to prove there's substance — then points to the whole atom. It teaches a little for free to earn the click; it never withholds everything (that's clickbait, and the audience punishes it).

## Structure

1. **The hook (post 1 / first 3 seconds).** The scar, sharpened to its most scroll-stopping form. A hard, specific, recognizable pain — ideally as a flat statement or a "this is why" promise. No "a thread 🧵" throat-clearing; the hook *is* the thread's reason to exist. This is 80% of performance — spend the most effort here.

2. **The turn (1–2 posts).** Name the fundamental and give *one* genuinely useful beat of the model — a real "oh" the reader can take away even if they never click. Free value builds the trust that earns the click. Keep it tight: one idea, concrete.

3. **The gap (1 post).** Make the remaining value visible without giving it: "That's one of N reasons this happens to you. The fix for all of them is the same move — and it's not what you think." Create curiosity honestly; don't lie about what's behind the link.

4. **The CTA (final post).** One link to the free lead magnet. Soft, confident: "Wrote the whole thing up — fix it on your own code today: [link]." One ask.

## Channel notes

- **X thread:** 5–9 posts. Each post readable alone. Hook post carries no link (reach penalty); link in the last post or first reply.
- **LinkedIn:** same beats, one post, line breaks for rhythm, link in comments.
- **Short video:** same beats as a script. Hook in first 3 seconds or it's dead. Add `[VISUAL: …]` cues. 30–60 seconds.

## Gate (plus `voice.md`'s)

- Does the **hook** stand completely alone and stop a scroll?
- Is there **one real takeaway** even for non-clickers? (No pure withholding.)
- Is the curiosity gap **honest** — the link delivers exactly what's implied?
- **One** CTA, one link, pointed at the **free** thing (never straight to the paid offer)?
