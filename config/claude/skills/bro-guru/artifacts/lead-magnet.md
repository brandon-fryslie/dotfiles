# Artifact: Lead Magnet

> One of four capped channel templates (lead-magnet · landing-page · social-thread · email). Each is the same underlying shape — **atom(s) × hook × CTA × channel-fit**. The lead magnet is the canonical/default. To add a channel, copy the nearest template; don't grow a fifth mode without a reason.

**What it is:** one whole atom, given away free, on the open web. The single most important artifact — it's both the proof the product is real and the top of the funnel. It must *over*-deliver. A grifter keeps this hollow; you make it a knockout on purpose.

**Length:** long enough to land all four atom beats and leave the reader fixed — usually 800–2000 words. Not longer for its own sake.

## Structure

1. **The scar (first 2 sentences).** Open cold on the PAIN, their words. No throat-clearing, no "in this post." They should think *that's me* before they've decided to keep reading. (`atom.md` beat 1)

2. **Twist the knife, briefly.** One short paragraph: name the cost of living with it — the compounding tax, the time bleed, the fear. This is real urgency, earned from consequence, not a countdown. Don't wallow; 3–4 sentences.

3. **The name.** Reveal the true fundamental. This is the relief beat — "this has a name, and people who know the name don't have your problem." Deliver it with confidence. (beat 2)

4. **The model.** The one idea that dissolves it, in plain language, minimum effective dose. Strip the jargon; keep the load-bearing core. One model, not five tips. Make it repeatable back. (beat 3)

5. **The before/after — on their world.** Show it. A concrete broken thing, then the same thing after the model, and the punchline: *notice it can't break the old way anymore.* Real example over clean fiction. This is where the win becomes felt. (beat 4)

6. **The whole-give close + one bridge.** Confirm they just got a real, complete win — no cliffhanger on the part that mattered. *Then* the single CTA: this was one wall; there are N more doing the same thing to you; the full thing takes them all down — [link]. Confident, not desperate. Exactly one ask.

## Gate (plus the shared one in `voice.md`)

- Could a reader **fix their actual problem** using only this piece? If no, it's a tease, not a lead magnet — fix it.
- Is the value delivered **before** the CTA, not gated behind it?
- One atom, not three crammed together?
