# Artifact: Email (sequence or broadcast)

> One of four capped channel templates. Covers both the **welcome/nurture sequence** (fired after someone grabs the lead magnet) and **one-off broadcasts**. Same unit: one email = one atom (or one beat of one atom), one CTA.

**What it is:** the channel that converts a free reader into a buyer over time, by delivering more real wins to an inbox until buying is the obvious next step. The list is the asset; every email either gives value or makes the offer, ideally both. One email never carries two atoms — that's two emails.

## The sequence shape (after lead-magnet opt-in)

The job of the sequence is to stack proof until the offer is a formality.

1. **Email 1 — deliver + over-deliver.** Hand over the lead magnet as promised, then add one bonus beat they didn't expect. Set the relationship: *I give real things.* No sell yet.
2. **Emails 2–4 — one fresh atom each.** A new scar named and partly dissolved per email, each ending in a soft bridge to the full product. They're getting wins free; the paid thing is just "all of them, faster." Value first, offer second.
3. **Email 5 — the offer, straight.** Make the case plainly: here's the full thing, here's who it's for, here's the price, here's the cost of staying stuck. Confident, honest, one CTA.
4. **Emails 6+ — objection + proof.** Each handles one real objection or shows one real before/after, always with the single CTA. Stop when it stops converting; don't nag.

## Single-email shape (each one)

1. **Subject line** — the scar or the curiosity, short, lowercase-honest over hype-caps. This is the open-rate; it's the thread's hook in `social-thread.md` form.
2. **Cold open** — one or two lines, straight into the pain or the story. No "Hope you're well!"
3. **One beat of value** — a usable idea, even for non-buyers.
4. **One CTA** — to the free thing (early) or the offer (later). Never two asks.
5. **A human P.S.** — often the most-read line; use it for the realest, least-polished thought.

## Gate (plus `voice.md`'s)

- **One atom, one CTA** per email — no doubling up?
- Does each email **give something** even to someone who never buys?
- Is the sell **earned** by value already delivered earlier in the sequence?
- Subject line honest — does the email deliver what the subject implied?
