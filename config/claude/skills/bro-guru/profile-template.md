# Product Profile — the single source of truth

This is the genre's anatomy (`concept.md`) **answered for one specific product**. Fill it once. Every piece of content derives from it and may not contradict it. Copy the block below into the product's repo as `PROFILE.md` and replace every `<…>`.

When bootstrapping: infer aggressively from the subject. Only ask the user for fields a competent expert genuinely couldn't guess — usually **audience precision**, **the buying moment**, and **the enemy**. Don't interrogate; propose a filled draft and let them correct it.

---

```markdown
# <Product> — Profile

## Subject
<The thing you know cold and can defend. One sentence.>

## Audience
<Not "developers." The specific person, their self-image, what they already
can do, and what they refuse to do. The refusal matters — it's why the
"proper" path failed them.>

## The Mark's Moment (the buying trigger)
<The precise instant of pain when they're ready to pay. Name it like a place.
If you can't describe the moment in one vivid sentence, you don't know your
buyer yet.>

## The Enemy
<The false belief or failed/dreaded alternative you position against. What
were they told that didn't work? What "proper" path are they avoiding?>

## The Promise (From → To)
<From <their current pain> To <their desired future>. Concrete. Time-bound if
honest. Not "learn X" — a claim about their future.>

## The Proof Mechanism
- **Free win:** <the one whole atom you give away that actually works>
- **Credibility:** <your real scars / artifacts / before-afters — no fabrication>

## Voice
- **Bro dial:** <0–10. See voice.md. swe4vibe-style honest-confident ≈ 6–7.>
- **Banned for this product:** <any moves off-limits for this specific audience>

## The Atom List (curriculum)
<Ordered. Each row: the scar (their words) → the true name → the model that
dissolves it. This IS the product. See atom.md for the unit.>
1. <scar> → <name> → <model>
2. …

## Offer Ladder
- **Free:** <which atom is the lead magnet>
- **Core:** <the stack — what they buy, rough form factor>
- **Premium:** <cohort / community / templates / 1:1 — same atoms, more access>

## Channels
<Where teasers live and which atom each leads with.>

## Open
<Deliberately-undecided calls: name, spine order, lead-magnet pick, pricing.
Listing them keeps the profile honest instead of fake-complete.>
```

## Worked example: swe4vibe

swe4vibe already instantiates this profile across two files in its repo — it just predates this template:

- **`VISION.md`** answers Subject, Audience, The Mark's Moment ("the wall"), The Enemy ("not a CS degree / the gurus are grifters"), The Promise, The Proof Mechanism (the free atom is devastating on purpose), Voice doctrine, and the Offer.
- **`ATOMS.md`** is the Atom List — and uniquely, every atom traces to a `[LAW:token]` in the universal-laws canon, which is *its* source of truth. That traceability is swe4vibe's version of "the substance is real": each lesson is a known engineering law, lowered into the symptom register.

When cutting content for swe4vibe, treat those two files as the profile. A fresh product gets a single `PROFILE.md` from the block above.
